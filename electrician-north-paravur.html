Questions people ask

Why does my MCB keep tripping?

Common causes are a faulty appliance, a damaged wire, moisture in a switch board or too much load on one circuit. Book a wiring fault diagnosis and our electrician will trace the cause and explain the fix before doing it.

Do you bring the fan, switch or light?

The price covers labour. You can buy the part yourself, or our electrician can bring it and you pay the shop price on top.

Can you fix a fan that runs slowly?

Yes. A slow fan usually needs a new capacitor, which is a short job covered under fan repair.
