Low Water Pressure in Taps: Common Reasons and Fixes

FixMate Kerala · 10 November 2026

മലയാളത്തിൽ വായിക്കുക

Weak flow from a tap or shower is common in homes with an overhead tank. Most causes are simple to find.

1. Is it one tap or the whole house?

If only one tap is slow, the problem is usually in that tap. If every tap is slow, look at the tank, the main pipe or the pump.

2. Blocked aerator or shower head

Hard water and silt collect in the small mesh at the tip of the tap and in shower heads. Unscrew it, rinse it and soak it in vinegar for an hour. This alone fixes many slow taps.

3. Low water level or dirty tank

With a gravity tank, pressure depends on how high and how full the tank is. Silt at the bottom of a dirty tank can also block the outlet. Cleaning the tank often improves flow.

4. Partly closed valve

Check that the valves near the tank and under the sink are fully open.

5. Air lock in the pipe

After the tank runs dry, air can get trapped in the pipes. Open all taps for a few minutes after refilling the tank to push the air out.

6. Old or leaking pipes

Old GI pipes rust inside and narrow over time. Hidden leaks also reduce pressure. Look for damp patches on walls or floors.

Tip: if the top floor always has weak flow, a small pressure pump can help. Ask a plumber to check the pipe size first.
