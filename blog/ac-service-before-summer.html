AC Service Before Summer: What Is Included and Why It Matters

FixMate Kerala · 10 November 2026

Kerala summers are hot and humid, and the AC works hardest from February to May. A service before summer keeps it cooling well and saves electricity.

How often to service

  • Basic service: every 3 to 4 months if you use the AC daily
  • Deep cleaning: once a year, ideally before summer
  • Filter wash: every 2 weeks during heavy use, which you can do yourself

What a basic service includes

  • Washing the filters
  • Cleaning the indoor coil and fins
  • Checking the drain pipe for blockage
  • Cleaning the outdoor unit
  • Checking cooling and current draw

What a deep cleaning adds

A jet wash of the indoor coil and blower, which removes dust and fungus that cause bad smell and weak airflow.

Signs your AC needs attention now

  • Cooling is weak even at the lowest temperature
  • Water is dripping from the indoor unit
  • A bad smell when you switch it on
  • Ice on the pipes or coil
  • The outdoor unit is noisy or keeps stopping
About gas refilling: an AC does not use up gas in normal working. If it needs gas, there is usually a leak. Ask for the leak to be found and fixed, not just a refill.
