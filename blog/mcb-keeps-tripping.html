MCB Keeps Tripping? Causes and What to Do

FixMate Kerala · 10 November 2026

മലയാളത്തിൽ വായിക്കുക

An MCB that trips again and again is a warning, not a nuisance. It is protecting your wiring and your family. Here is how to find the likely cause.

Safety first: never tape, block or bypass an MCB or ELCB, and never replace it with a higher rating to stop the tripping.

1. Too much load on one circuit

Running an iron, a water heater and a mixer on the same circuit can overload it. If the MCB trips only when several heavy appliances run together, spread them across different points or ask an electrician to add a separate circuit.

2. A faulty appliance

Switch off and unplug everything on that circuit. Turn the MCB back on, then plug appliances in one at a time. If it trips when one item is switched on, that appliance is the likely cause.

3. Moisture after rain

Water in an outdoor socket, a motor switch or a wall with damp often trips the ELCB. If tripping starts after heavy rain, do not keep switching it on. Moisture in wiring needs an electrician.

4. Damaged or old wiring

Rats, old insulation and loose connections can cause short circuits. Signs include a burning smell, warm switch boards and flickering lights.

5. A weak or faulty MCB

MCBs can wear out. An electrician can test it and replace it with the correct rating.

When to call an electrician

  • It trips even with everything unplugged
  • You smell burning or see marks on a switch board
  • It started after rain or water leakage
  • A switch board or plug feels warm
